1. Which temple has the famous 66-metre vimana and is a UNESCO World Heritage site?

Tip: Brihadeeswarar's vimana (≈66 m) was the tallest structure of its time. UNESCO 2004.

2. Pongal (Thai Pongal) is a festival of…

Tip: Pongal thanks the sun and the harvest; the pot "overflowing" (pongam) is the symbol.

3. The Jambai inscription is famous because it identifies…

Tip: Jambai (Tamil-Brahmi) names Atiyan Neduman Anji, a Satyaputra — solving a 2,000-year question.

4. Which classical dance was born in the Tamil temples?

Tip: Bharatanatyam grew out of temple sadir in Tamil Nadu.

5. Kanchipuram is world-famous for its…

Tip: Kanchipuram silk with real zari and a bold temple border is a national icon.

6. "நன்றி" (nandri) means…

Tip: நன்றி = thank you.

7. Chithirai Thiruvizha is the 21-day wedding festival of…

Tip: Chithirai is Madurai's grand 21-day goddess wedding festival.

8. The sweet name of filter coffee in a Tamil home is…

Tip: "Kaapi!" — frothed between two tumblers.

9. Kanda Vial, a famous Madurai dish, is made mainly from…

Tip: Kanda = banana flower, slow-cooked with lentils and coconut.

10. Vaikuntavasi (the golden festival) is celebrated at the temple in…

Tip: Vaikuntavasi is the holiest Vaishnava festival at Srirangam's Ranganathaswamy temple.
