Frequently asked questions

Everything about courses, certificates, workshops and the AI tutor. Can’t find it? Ask a teacher.

Getting started

QDo I need to know any Tamil to start?

No. Level 1 starts from the alphabet itself — உயிர் எழுத்துக்கள் (vowels) and மெய் எழுத்துக்கள் (consonants). Many of our learners start from zero.

QHow long until I can hold a basic conversation?

Most consistent learners can introduce themselves, order food and handle simple travel situations after Levels 1–2 (about 4–6 weeks at 20 minutes a day).

Certificates

QWhat does the 90% certificate rule mean?

For workshops, attendance_percentage = attended required minutes ÷ total required minutes × 100. You need 90% or more. This is calculated by the platform from attendance records — not by the AI.

QHow are certificates verified?

Every certificate has a unique ID (for example TN-2026-000001) and a QR code. Anyone can check it at /verify/{certificate ID}. Revoked certificates are shown as invalid.

Languages

QCan I learn in my own language?

Yes. Explanations are available in English, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada. Set your native language in your profile and the AI tutor will use it when helpful.

AI tutor

QWhat is the AI Tamil Tutor?

A floating assistant available everywhere. It answers from the platform content (RAG), sees which lesson you are reading, does vocabulary lookups and gives practice sentences. It never invents workshop dates or attendance.

QCan I use my own OpenRouter API key (BYOAI)?

Yes, if the platform allows it. The key is encrypted at rest, only used server-side for your requests, never displayed in full, and can be deleted anytime.

General

QIs Solai free?

Courses and the core learning experience are free. Some live workshops are paid — when a payment provider is configured, registration shows PENDING/PAID states clearly and never fakes a payment.
