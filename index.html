Your learning path

Six levels that take you from sounds to professional Tamil

  1. Level 1

    Absolute Beginner

    உயிர் எழுத்துக்கள் & மெய் எழுத்துக்கள், sounds, greetings, numbers.

  2. Level 2

    Everyday Tamil

    Introductions, family, food, shopping, transport, time and common verbs.

  3. Level 3

    Conversation

    Restaurant, railway station, bus, hotel, hospital, office and friends.

  4. Level 4

    Grammar

    Sentence structure, tense, pronouns, negation, adjectives, particles.

  5. Level 5

    Reading & Writing

    Read words and sentences, write practice, short passages.

  6. Level 6

    Advanced / Professional

    Formal Tamil, professional communication and cultural context.
